# Quick Deployment Guide - Make Your Website Live

## 🚀 EASIEST METHOD: Netlify Drag & Drop (Free)

### Step 1: Go to Netlify
1. Open your browser
2. Go to: https://www.netlify.com/
3. Click "Sign up" (it's free!)
4. Sign up with GitHub, Google, or email

### Step 2: Deploy Your Website
1. Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard
2. Look for the area that says "Drag and drop your site output folder here"
3. Open your file explorer
4. Go to: `C:\Users\drpal\shanker-homeo-stores`
5. **Drag the entire folder** onto the Netlify dashboard
6. Wait for upload to complete (usually 1-2 minutes)

### Step 3: Get Your URL
1. After upload, Netlify will show you your new site
2. Your URL will look like: `https://shanker-homeo-stores-xxxxx.netlify.app`
3. Copy this URL - this is your live website address!

### Step 4: Test Your Website
1. Click on your site URL
2. Your website is now live!
3. Share this URL with anyone
